neptune

Neptune is the farthest official planet from the Sun and is another ice giant with a deep blue appearance. It is known for extremely strong winds, some of the fastest ever recorded in the Solar System. Neptune has dark storm systems similar to Jupiter’s Great Red Spot and several moons, including Triton, which orbits in the opposite direction of the planet’s rotation. Because it is so far away, Neptune receives very little sunlight and is extremely cold.
