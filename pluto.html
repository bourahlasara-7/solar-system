pluto

Pluto was once considered the ninth planet but is now classified as a dwarf planet. It is located in the Kuiper Belt, a distant region filled with icy objects beyond Neptune. Pluto is small, cold, and made mostly of rock and ice. It has a thin atmosphere that expands when it gets closer to the Sun and freezes when it moves farther away. Pluto has several moons, including Charon, which is so large compared to Pluto that they almost orbit each other like a double system.
