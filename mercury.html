mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest official planet in the Solar System. It has a rocky surface filled with craters that resemble the Moon because it has almost no atmosphere to protect it from meteors. Days on Mercury are extremely hot while nights become freezing cold due to the lack of atmosphere. A year on Mercury is very short because it orbits the Sun in only 88 Earth days. Despite being closest to the Sun, it is not the hottest planet.
