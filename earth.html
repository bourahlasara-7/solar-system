earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet that supports life. About 71% of its surface is covered by water, and its atmosphere contains oxygen that living organisms need to survive. Earth has diverse environments including oceans, forests, deserts, and polar ice caps. It has one natural moon that affects tides and helps stabilize the planet’s rotation. Earth’s balanced temperature and protective atmosphere make it unique among the planets.
