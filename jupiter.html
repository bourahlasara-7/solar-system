jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System and is made mostly of hydrogen and helium gas. It is famous for the Great Red Spot, a gigantic storm that has lasted for centuries. Jupiter has dozens of moons, including Ganymede, the largest moon in the Solar System. Its powerful gravity influences many objects around it and helps protect inner planets by attracting comets and asteroids. Even though it is huge, Jupiter has no solid surface like Earth.
