uranus

Uranus is a cold ice giant with a pale blue-green color caused by methane gas in its atmosphere. Unlike most planets, Uranus rotates on its side, making it appear to roll around the Sun. This unusual tilt may have been caused by a massive collision long ago. The planet has faint rings and many moons, and temperatures there are among the coldest in the Solar System. Uranus was the first planet discovered using a telescope.
