Mars

Mars is known as the “Red Planet” because iron oxide, or rust, covers much of its surface. It has giant volcanoes, deep valleys, polar ice caps, and frequent dust storms. Mars is colder than Earth, but scientists believe liquid water may have existed there long ago. It has two small moons named Phobos and Deimos. Because of its similarities to Earth, Mars is one of the main targets in the search for past or future extraterrestrial life.
