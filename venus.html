venus

Venus is often called Earth’s “sister planet” because it is similar in size and structure, but its environment is extremely hostile. Thick clouds of carbon dioxide trap heat through a powerful greenhouse effect, making Venus the hottest planet in the Solar System. Its surface is covered with volcanic plains and mountains, and the pressure there is strong enough to crush spacecraft. Venus also rotates very slowly and in the opposite direction compared to most planets.
