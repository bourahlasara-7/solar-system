the sun

extremely hot hydrogen and helium that produces energy through nuclear fusion in its core, creating the light and heat that make life on Earth possible. The Sun’s gravity keeps all the planets, moons, asteroids, and comets orbiting around it. Its surface temperature is about 5,500°C, while the core reaches millions of degrees. Without the Sun, the Solar System would be cold, dark, and lifeless.
